**North American Creator-Led Premium Brands**

- Direct-to-consumer apparel and accessories

- Brand partnerships and collaborations

**ESTIMATE:** Premium lifestyle spending represents 3-5% of discretionary income for this demographic, suggesting $60-100 billion annual addressable spend.
